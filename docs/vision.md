# UFAS Cortex

## Vision

UFAS Cortex is a modern educational platform designed exclusively for medical students at the Faculty of Medicine, University Ferhat Abbas Setif 1.

The platform centralizes all academic resources in one place, including previous exams, lecture notes, summaries, and study materials.

Its long-term vision is to become the primary digital ecosystem for medical students by integrating artificial intelligence, collaborative learning, and personalized study tools.

## Target Audience

- First Year Medical Students
- Second Year Medical Students
- Third Year Medical Students
- Fourth Year Medical Students
- Fifth Year Medical Students
- Sixth Year Medical Students

## Mission

Learn More. Stress Less.
